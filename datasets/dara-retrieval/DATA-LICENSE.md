**Changes for this release.** MINEA selected the 53 single-order retrieval windows from the documented scenarios, converted the inclusive frame numbers, and grouped consecutive equal revised CC10 labels into intervals. The exporter attached worker and order context, higher process levels and spatial trajectories, assigned the replay's completion-area source, and represented the result as CSV and XES with synthetic timestamp origins. The log is an adaptation published by the MINEA project. [Conversion details](README.md)
