**Changes for this release.** MINEA selected the 53 single-order retrieval windows from the documented scenarios, converted the inclusive frame numbers, and grouped consecutive frames with the same revised CC10 activity label into observations. The exporter attached worker and order context, higher process levels and spatial trajectories, assigned each observation to the source for its ending area in the replay, and represented the result as CSV and XES with synthetic timestamp origins. The neighborhood figure uses ground truth activity labels to show votes in order of classifier distance. Its CSV includes the distances computed from the wearable readings and interval durations. The experiment package also includes the sensor synchronization anchors, candidate activities and scores derived from BLE and IMU readings, constraints mined from the ground truth activity sequences, their validation rankings, and the activity selections for each order. These adaptations are published by the MINEA project. [Conversion details](README.md) · [Experiment records and reproduction guide](../../experiments/dara/README.md)

**Warehouse layout and route.** The warehouse maps and zone geometry adapt Figure 2.42 (page 54) of *DaRA Documentation* by Friedrich Niemann and Fernando Moya Rueda. The layout was aligned with the fixed-beacon plan in Figure 7.1 and positions in Table 7.2. We redrew and labeled the zones and overlaid the recorded zone visits and ground truth activities of worker S04. The map arrow connects representative points in successive recorded zones. The animation highlights the recorded zone throughout each activity.
